## Severity Guide

- Critical: blocks login, purchase, payment, order confirmation, or exposes sensitive data.
- High: causes incorrect price, savings, product selection, payment choice, or major mobile/browser break.
- Medium: degrades important journey but workaround exists.
- Low: minor usability issue with limited business impact.
